# 🍞 Bake Plan — "Mum's Balance Loaf"

**Order:** pickup **Sat 19 Sep, 11:00 AM**, pre-sliced · tin loaf · no seeds · cold-proof method.

Target: one ~645 g loaf, 25% wholemeal (lighter than bake #2's 37%).

## Recipe

**Levain (~70 g):** 20 g starter + 25 g bread flour + 25 g water

**Dough:**
| Ingredient | g |
|---|---|
| Bread flour | 235 |
| Wholemeal flour | 90 |
| Water | 220 |
| Levain | 70 |
| Salt | 6.5 |
| Olive oil | 12 |
| Honey | 12 |

Total flour 360 g · hydration ~71% · wholemeal 25% · levain ~19% · salt 1.8% · dough ≈ 645 g.

---

## Friday 18 Sep — prep & cold proof

- [ ] **Fed keeper** — keep ~20–30 g starter, feed 1:1:1, ~2 h room temp, then fridge.
- [ ] **1:00 PM — build levain** (20 g starter + 25 g flour + 25 g water).
- [ ] **~4:00 PM — mix + autolyse** (flours + water + levain, rest 30 min).
- [ ] **4:30 PM — add salt, oil, honey** → mix in.
- [ ] **5:00 PM — Fold #1**
- [ ] **5:30 PM — Fold #2**
- [ ] **6:00 PM — Fold #3** (smooth, domed, glossy).
- [ ] **~7:00 PM — bulk** until puffy & domed (~40–50%).
- [ ] **~8:00 PM — shape** into the oiled tin (taut, even roll, seam down).
- [ ] **+20–40 min — short room-temp proof** (slightly puffed, NOT to the rim).
- [ ] **~9:00 PM — cover & fridge** (cold proof overnight).

## Saturday 19 Sep — bake straight from cold

- [ ] **Preheat 210 °C + steam tray** (≥30 min; aim to bake by ~8:30 AM).
- [ ] **Take tin from fridge** — do NOT warm it.
- [ ] **Score** one confident centre slash.
- [ ] **Bake 210 °C / 15 min** (with steam).
- [ ] **190 °C / ~45 min**, remove steam tray, rotate.
- [ ] **Done:** deep golden, hollow tap, internal **93–96 °C** (tent with foil if darkening).
- [ ] **Cool on a rack ~1.5–2 h.**
- [ ] **~10:45 AM — slice & bag.**
- [ ] **11:00 AM — pickup.** 🎉

---

## Notes
- More white flour = less absorbent → may feel wetter; hold back ~10 g water if needed.
- Keep it mild — don't over-proof before chilling.
- Shape with wet hands + a scraper.
- No instant yeast needed.
