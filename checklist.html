Friday 18 Sep — prep & cold proof

Feed the keeperKeep ~20–30 g of starter; feed 1:1:1; ~2 h at room temp; then fridge.
1:00 PMBuild the levain20 g starter + 25 g bread flour + 25 g water → ~70 g. Cover, room temp.
~4:00 PMMix + autolyse235 g bread flour + 90 g wholemeal + 220 g water + 70 g levain. Rest 30 min.
4:30 PMAdd salt, oil, honey6.5 g salt + 12 g olive oil + 12 g honey. Mix in. No seeds.
5:00 PMFold #1Wet hands. Stretch & fold or coil fold.
5:30 PMFold #2
6:00 PMFold #3Dough should be smooth, domed, glossy.
~7:00 PMBulk until puffyWait for ~40–50% rise, domed & jiggly. (29 °C → fast.)
~8:00 PMShape into the tinTaut, even roll. Seam down. Build top tension.
+20–40 minShort room-temp proofJust until slightly puffed — NOT to the rim.
~9:00 PMCover & into the FRIDGECold proof overnight (~10–14 h). Done for the night ✅

Saturday 19 Sep — bake straight from cold

≥30 min beforePreheat 210 °C + steam trayWater in a tray on the lower shelf. Aim to bake by ~8:30 AM.
Take tin from fridgeDo NOT warm it — bake straight from cold.
ScoreOne confident centre slash (cold dough scores easily).
Bake 210 °C / 15 minWith steam. Don't open the door.
Drop to 190 °C / ~45 minRemove steam tray, rotate loaf. Cold start = a few min longer.
Check doneDeep golden, hollow tap, internal 93–96 °C. Tent with foil if top darkens early.
Cool on a rack ~1.5–2 hFully cool before slicing.
~10:45 AMSlice & bagEven ~1.5 cm slices. Wrap soon so it doesn't dry.
11:00 AMPickup 🎉
All done — good luck with the order! 🍞
Notes: More white flour = less absorbent → dough may feel wetter; hold back ~10 g water if needed. Wholemeal 25% (lighter than bake #2). Keep it mild — don't over-proof before chilling.
