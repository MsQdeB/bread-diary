Shared levain: 30 g starter + 40 g bread flour + 40 g water = 110 g. Divide the finished dough: ~800 g → large, ~200 g → small. Both ≈ 71% hydration, 25% wholemeal.

Prep & mix

Build the shared levain30 g starter + 40 g bread flour + 40 g water (=110 g). Ready ~3 h at 29 °C.
Feed the keeperKeep some starter back; feed 1:1:1; ~2 h room temp; fridge.
Whisk levain + water + honey + oilDissolve the levain, honey and oil in the water FIRST (they mix far better this way).
Add flours → autolyse363 g bread + 138 g wholemeal. Rest 30 min.
Mix in salt (pincer/Rubaud)Salt 10 g dissolved in a splash of water. Mix thoroughly NOW while shaggy — pincer (pinch+rotate) or Rubaud (stretch-slap). No tearing.
Fold in the chia (whole dough)Gently with 1–2 folds (lamination) — don't squeeze.
Optional: pinch of instant yeast1–2 g ONLY if the levain looked sluggish/flat. Skip if it was domed & lively.

Folds & bulk

+0 minFold #1Wet hands. Stretch & fold or coil fold.
+30 minFold #2
+60 minFold #3Dough smooth, domed, glossy.
Bulk, then FRIDGE overnightFold in the chia (whole dough), then ~2 folds, then cover & refrigerate for a COLD BULK overnight.

Morning — divide, seed, shape, proof

Take the dough out of the fridgeCold bulk is done. Cold dough is much less sticky — shape it cold.
Divide the dough~800 g → large · ~200 g → small.
Add FLAX+PUMPKIN to the SMALL loaf~16 g cracked/soaked. Press flat, scatter, fold in. (Chia was already mixed into both doughs.)
Shape LARGE → 20×10×9 tinTaut even roll, seam down. Oil the tin.
Shape SMALL → 13.5×6.5×3 tinGently; shallow tin (loaf rises above the rim). Oil it well.
Final proof at room tempCover; 30–45 min at 29 °C. Ready when domed + a poke springs back slowly. Don't over-proof.

0–15 minLarge in @ 210 °CDon't open the door.

~+25 minSMALL loaf out~20–25 min in oven. Check internal 93–96 °C. Tent with foil if it browns early.
~55 minLARGE loaf outTotal ~55 min. Deep golden, hollow tap, internal 93–96 °C.

Cool & finish

Cool both on a rackLarge ~1–2 h, small ~1 h. Fully cool before slicing.
Slice & enjoy 🎉
All done — enjoy the two loaves! 🍞
Notes: One dough for both tins (same hydration/wholemeal). Mix one bowl. Chia in BOTH loaves (mixed into the whole dough) · flax+pumpkin → small loaf at the divide. Method: COLD BULK overnight → divide/shape/proof in the morning. If the levain seemed sluggish, a pinch (1–2 g) of instant yeast is harmless insurance. Mixing tip: mix salt/oil/honey/chia in THOROUGHLY while the dough is still shaggy (pincer/Rubaud). Once it's a smooth mass, use FOLDS only — never squeeze/rip. Small shallow tin browns fast — watch it. No scoring on the small loaf needed (optional).
