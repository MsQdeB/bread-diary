~72% effective hydration, 25% wholemeal. All 290 g water goes into the dough (no seed soak). Why 290 not 276: the dry seeds absorb some, so +14 g keeps the dough from going stiff. NO oil, NO honey.
⚑ Fixes from bake #9: ① TRUE AUTOLYSE — flour + water only, then levain + salt ② BULK to 40–50% — don't shape early ③ if the cold loaf hasn't risen, WARM-PROOF before baking ④ NO big water tray in the oven (it crashed the temp) ⑤ oil the tin AND dust with flour ⑥ bake deep golden, ~70 min.

Friday — levain, true autolyse & prep

4:00 PMBuild the levain30 g starter + 30 g bread flour + 30 g water (= 90 g). 1:1:1 · 29 °C → peaks ~2.5–3 h (~6:45 PM).
anytimeRoast the seedsFlax 15 + pumpkin 20 — roast dry in a pan/oven until fragrant. Cool. No soaking. Added dry at fold #1.
~6:50 PMTRUE AUTOLYSE (flours + water only)290 g bread + 110 g wholemeal + 290 g water (all of it). Mix to shaggy — NO knead. NO salt, NO levain. Lid on, rest 45–60 min.
~7:45 PMAdd levain + salt87 g levain + 8 g salt (dissolve in a splash of water). Fold gently to incorporate — don't knead.
~8:00 PMRest 15–20 minLet the levain + salt settle before folding.
~8:15 PMFold #1 — laminate the ROASTED seedsStretch to a rectangle, scatter the dry roasted flax + pumpkin thinly (hold some back), letter-fold, rotate 90°, scatter the rest, fold. Wet hands, no knead.
~8:45 PMFold #2
~9:15 PMFold #3
~9:15–10:30 PMBulk to 40–50%Puffy, domed & jiggly. DON'T shape early (#9 mistake). 29 °C = fast — watch it.
OIL the tin + DUST with flourDon't skip the flour.
~10:30 PMShape → tin (bench scraper)Drag-and-round for a taut skin, then carpet roll · seal every fold · seam DOWN · tuck ends. Wet hands, no bench flour.
~10:45 PMCover → FRIDGECold proof overnight.

Saturday — bake & finish

AMCHECK the risePuffy & domed? Poke springs back slowly? If not risen → WARM-PROOF at room temp (1–4 h at ~30 °C) until ready. Don't bake a cold, flat loaf.
−30 minPreheat 210 °COven thermometer in the centre — confirm it really is ~210. NO big water tray.
ScoreOne confident centre slash.
Bake 210 °C / 15 minSteam optional: a few ice cubes on the oven floor — NOT a big tray under the loaf.
→190 °C / 45 minRotate. Tent foil if the top darkens early.
→190 °C / +10 minDeep golden — don't pull pale.
Done checkDeep golden, hollow tap, internal 93–96 °C.
~1–2 hCool, slice + crumb shot 🎉
Bake #10 done — good luck! 🍞
