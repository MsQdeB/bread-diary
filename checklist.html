68% hydration, 25% wholemeal. Water 258 g total = ~50 g for soaking seeds + ~208 g into the dough. Oil/honey are NOT water.
⚑ Apply bake #7 fixes: ① oil the tin AND dust with flour (or line with parchment) ② even, taut shaping ③ mix less, fold more ④ bake to 93–96 °C — don't pull pale.

Saturday 10 Oct — prep & cold proof

Feed the keeperKeep some starter back; feed 1:1:1; ~2 h; fridge.
2:30 PMBuild the levain20 g starter + 35 g bread flour + 35 g water (=90 g). 27 °C room → slower; peaks ~3.5–4.5 h (~6–7 PM).
Soak seedsFlax 15 + pumpkin 20 (cracked) in ~50 g water (from the 258 g). 20–30 min.
~7:00 PMMix + autolyseLevain → ~208 g water → add 290 g bread + 110 g wholemeal. Rest 30 min.
~7:30 PMSalt, oil, honey + seeds8 g salt + 15 g oil + 15 g honey — mix only until incorporated. Fold in the soaked seeds EVENLY.
~7:45 PMFold #1Wet hands. Don't knead.
~8:15 PMFold #2
~8:45 PMFold #3
~9:15 PMBulk until puffy~40–50% rise, domed & jiggly (30 °C = fast).
OIL the tin + DUST with flourDon't skip the flour — it stops sticking (bake #7 lesson).
~9:45 PMShape → tinCARPET ROLL · even taut tension · seal every fold · seam DOWN · tuck ends.
~10:00 PMCover → FRIDGECold proof overnight. Sleep early ✅
